Privacy

Last updated: August 2026

VeriLink is a simple tool: paste a URL, it gets checked, you see the result. Here's exactly what happens to your data.

What we don't collect

We don't require an account, don't use cookies, and don't log or store the URLs you submit. Each scan is processed and forgotten — nothing is saved to a database on our end.

Third-party services

To check a URL, we send it to two external security services: Google Safe Browsing and VirusTotal. This is the entire point of the tool — that's how the threat check works. Each service processes the URL under its own privacy policy, which we don't control.

Basic server logs

Our hosting provider (Render) may keep standard technical logs (like IP addresses and request timestamps) for a short period, purely for abuse prevention and uptime monitoring — this is standard infrastructure practice, not something VeriLink itself collects or has access to for tracking purposes.
